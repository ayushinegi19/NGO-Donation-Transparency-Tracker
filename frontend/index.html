Every donation, accounted for.

Every donation and every expenditure is recorded on the blockchain. Anyone can verify how much a cause raised, exactly how it was spent, and what remains — no one, not even the NGO, can edit the record.

  • On-chain donations recorded against each donor's wallet
  • Itemized spending with SHA-256 receipt proofs
  • Automatic audit — every payment needs a matching, never-reused receipt
  • Refunds if a goal is missed by the deadline
  • Publicly verifiable — no wallet needed to view

Verify a cause creator

After checking documents off-chain, record who is behind a wallet: a registered organisation (NGO, trust, society; check registration and FCRA papers) or an individual raising money for a personal cause such as medical treatment (check ID and supporting documents, e.g. hospital estimate).
